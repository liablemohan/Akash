I'm the voice behind Banarasi Bhaukal.

A page born from pure love for Kashi — one of the oldest living cities in the world.

Through photos, reels, and stories, I share the temples, the ghats, the chai stalls, the chaos, and the calm.

This is Banaras: devotion and swagger, walking side by side.

"Bhaukal" isn't just a word here. It's an attitude.

The quiet confidence of a city that never needs to prove itself.

My content is a tribute to that energy — Mahadev's blessings, sunrise on the ghats, and the everyday moments outsiders rarely see.
